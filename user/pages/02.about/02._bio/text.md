## 30 Years, 200+ Implementations

I'm Amer Saeed, an Odoo Functional Consultant. I've spent three decades in the IT industry — long enough to watch ERP go from mainframe green-screens to cloud platforms you can configure in an afternoon, and to learn that the technology was never really the hard part.

The hard part is translating how a business actually runs — its exceptions, its workarounds, its "we've always done it this way" — into a system people will actually use. That's the work behind more than 200 ERP implementations: sitting with finance teams, warehouse staff, and plant managers to map the real process, then configuring Odoo to fit it instead of the other way around.

Today that work runs through **OdoCore**, an Odoo Silver Partner where I lead functional consulting across ERP, data, cloud, and digitization engagements for clients spanning manufacturing, healthcare, retail, trading, and services — from Pakistan to Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
