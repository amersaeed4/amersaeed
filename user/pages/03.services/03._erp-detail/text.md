## Odoo ERP, Covered End to End

Odoo implementations I lead span the full suite — **CRM, Sales, POS, Subscriptions**, and **Rental** on the commerce side; **Inventory, Manufacturing, Purchase, Maintenance**, and **Quality** in operations; **Recruitment, Time Off, Appraisals**, and **Payroll** in HR; plus **Website, eCommerce, Marketing Automation, Helpdesk, Field Service**, and more.

The process is the same regardless of module: configuration tailored to how you actually operate, careful data migration, real training for the people who'll use it daily, and support that doesn't disappear after go-live.
